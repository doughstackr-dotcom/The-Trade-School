Craft notes — independent of the theme

These are the fixes worth making whichever direction you pick.

One Appearance panelReplace the three-state sun/moon toggle with a real sheet: theme cards, motion, CRT strength and chart palette in one place.
Two theme systems, one ownerCabinet skins already re-token the app behind credits. Decide whether whole-app themes are free identity or paid economy — do not ship two pickers.
Turn the CRT downThe scanline overlay covers every route at 4.5%. It softens 9–11px mono labels. Make it optional and cap it at 2–3%.
Quiet the tickerA marquee at the top of every page competes with the hero. A static line with a “Details” link keeps the disclaimer visible and calmer.
Tokenize the chromeRadii range 6–16px plus pills and shadows are per-component. Add surface-3, line-strong, radius-card and shadow-card so a theme is a token file.
Protect the semanticsBull and bear keep their meaning in every direction; theme only the chrome. Also set the theme-color meta tag per theme.
